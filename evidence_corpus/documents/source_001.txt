[DEMO DATA] IMD OFFICIAL BULLETIN - WEATHER CONDITIONS & FLASH FLOODS REPORT

Severe rainfall triggered by an active low-pressure depression across the Bay of Bengal led to intense precipitation across Chennai and neighbouring districts on Thursday. Meteorological stations at Meenambakkam and Nungambakkam recorded 142mm and 128mm of rainfall within a continuous six-hour window.

The torrential downpour caused localized flash floods and significant water stagnation in key arterial routes, including parts of Velachery, Madipakkam, and low-lying neighbourhoods of South Chennai. Public transport services experienced temporary disruptions, and civic authorities deployed high-capacity water pumps to clear inundated subway underpasses.

Meteorological forecasters advised residents in coastal zones to avoid non-essential travel while stormwater drainage maintenance crews continued clearing road blockages throughout the night.
