[DEMO DATA] STATE DISASTER MANAGEMENT SITUATION REPORT

In response to heavy monsoon rains across coastal districts, four units of the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and three State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) battalions were mobilized across Greater Chennai on Thursday.

Rescue teams equipped with inflatable rubber boats and emergency de-watering pump sets were deployed as a precautionary measure in flood-prone corridors of T. Nagar, Velachery, and Tambaram. Over 240 personnel assisted local municipal staff in clearing fallen tree branches and assisting elderly residents in waterlogged apartment complexes.

No casualties or major building collapses were reported. The emergency control room confirmed that water levels receded swiftly by early Friday morning following the opening of secondary drainage sluice gates.
