[DEMO DATA] OFFICIAL PRESS COMMUNIQUÉ - SOCIÉTÉ DE LA TOUR EIFFEL

The management of the Eiffel Tower (SETE) categorically refutes sensational claims and synthetic videos circulating on digital networks alleging that the Eiffel Tower was struck by an astronomical meteorite or experienced structural collapse.

The monument is completely intact, operational, and welcoming visitors according to standard operating hours. Visual analysis of the viral video clip indicates it was produced using 3D visual effects software (CGI simulation) and subsequently distributed without context.

Visitors holding reservations are assured of standard security protocols and unrestricted access across all three observation decks.
