[DEMO DATA] MULTICENTRE DOUBLE-BLIND PHASE 3 CLINICAL TRIAL REPORT

A randomized, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial evaluating a bivalent mRNA candidate targeting respiratory syncytial virus and influenza strains enrolled 14,800 adult participants across 42 clinical investigation sites.

The primary endpoint of preventing medically attended lower respiratory tract illness was achieved, demonstrating a vaccine efficacy of 89.4% (95% CI: 84.1% - 93.2%) against confirmed infection. Neutralizing antibody titers showed robust seroconversion across all age cohorts, including participants aged 65 and older.

Reported adverse events were predominantly mild to moderate, consisting primarily of transient injection-site tenderness, fatigue, and low-grade pyrexia resolving within 48 hours. No treatment-related serious adverse events or pericarditis incidences were observed during the six-month follow-up period.
