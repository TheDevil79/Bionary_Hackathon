[DEMO DATA] MARITIME GEOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT & SUBSEA FEASIBILITY STUDY

High-resolution multibeam bathymetric and sub-bottom profiling surveys were conducted across the Palk Strait between Tamil Nadu and Northern Sri Lanka to evaluate maritime geomorphology and sediment transport dynamics.

The survey revealed shallow limestone shoals, calcareous sandstone ridges, and highly mobile calcarenite sediment drifts subject to intense tidal currents through the Pamban channel. The seabed stratification demonstrates that loose Holocene sedimentary deposits exceed 25 meters in thickness across the central depression.

The geotechnical report determined that submerged vehicular or railway tunnels beneath the strait are technically unfeasible without massive deep-trench dredging and extensive rock stabilization. No subterranean excavations or underwater tunnel projects have ever been commenced in this marine zone.
